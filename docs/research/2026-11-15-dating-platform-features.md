# Dating platform feature research — 15 November 2026

Research snapshot checked **15 November 2026**, using official help centers and
first-party announcements for Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, and OkCupid. This records
documented behavior, not hands-on qualification, adoption, effectiveness, or a
claim that every feature is available in the Philippines. A currently reachable
help page can describe a test, phased rollout, older interface, or temporarily
unavailable feature. Dates below are the publisher's displayed updates where
available; otherwise only the access date is established.

This document does not inventory Bondi's implementation or authorize changes.
The user's sequencing is to improve existing reliability, privacy, and UI first,
then account/authentication capabilities, then select a bounded feature addition.
Recommendations below follow that order.

## Tinder evidence

- **Profile editing:** The editor supports photos/Loops, bio, interests, lifestyle
  tags, intentions and other details on the app and web. Available fields vary by
  location. Name and age cannot be edited through the ordinary editor. The separate
  Interests guide describes adding, removing and ordering interests, and warns
  that the available choices vary geographically. Both guides were updated
  22 October 2026. [Editing your profile](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003339043-Editing-your-profile),
  [Interests](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360046122691-Interests).
- **Authentication and recovery:** Account creation documents verified phone
  numbers, email when prompted, and Apple/Google connections; some methods can be
  temporarily unavailable in particular markets. Changed-number recovery starts
  with the account's email through the login-help option. These are evidence of
  phone/provider recovery, not evidence of a Tinder-specific change-password
  screen. Both guides were updated 22 October 2026.
  [Create a Tinder Account](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003356706-Create-a-Tinder-Account),
  [Updating your phone number](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005147211-Updating-your-phone-number).
- **Pause and visibility:** Turning Discovery off hides a profile from new people
  while retaining existing match conversations; previously sent Likes can still
  produce matches. The subscription guide lists Incognito within Plus, inherited
  by Gold and Platinum. These are distinct controls, not account deletion. Guides
  updated 22 October 2026. [Discovery Settings](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003340963-Discovery-Settings),
  [Tinder subscriptions](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004487406-Tinder-subscriptions).
- **Blocking and reporting:** Profile blocking prevents mutual visibility, is
  irreversible, and is explicitly still a test unavailable to some users
  (22 October 2026). Reporting is available from profiles and matches on app/web,
  with reasons and details; the reporting guide describes confidentiality subject
  to legal exceptions (27 December 2024). [Block a Profile](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/12425789312013-Block-a-Profile),
  [Reporting profiles and content](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003822043-Reporting-profiles-and-content).
- **Notifications:** Users can manage email and push settings inside Tinder, with
  separate iOS, Android and browser controls. Guide updated 22 October 2026.
  [Managing your notifications](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003340683-Managing-your-notifications).
- **Matching explanations:** Tinder publishes general recommendation inputs,
  including activity, proximity, stated interests, photo cues and Likes/Nopes;
  this is not a per-candidate explanation of an individual result
  (22 October 2026). Your Type summarizes recent liking patterns but is limited
  to selected Android/iOS markets (17 September 2026). Neither source establishes
  a universally available prompt or like-with-comment feature.
  [The Method Behind Our Matching](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/7606685697037-Powering-Tinder-The-Method-Behind-Our-Matching),
  [Your Type](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/48975193510925-Your-Type).
- **Verification and date safety:** Photo Verification compares a video selfie
  with profile photos and explicitly does not guarantee identity or safety.
  Optional ID + Photo Verification is restricted to selected test markets on
  iOS, Android and web; its guide also disclaims safety guarantees. Both updated
  22 October 2026. Share My Date was announced on 22 April 2024 for sharing editable
  plans by link, with a named rollout-country list that did not include the
  Philippines; that announcement does not prove today's local availability.
  [Photo Verification processing](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/4422771431309-How-Does-Photo-Verification-Work),
  [ID + Photo Verification](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/19868368795917-ID-Photo-Verification),
  [Share My Date announcement](https://www.tinderpressroom.com/2024-04-22-SHORTCUT-YOUR-DATING-ADMIN-THANKS-TO-TINDERS-SHARE-MY-DATE).

## Bumble evidence

- **Profile editing:** App guides document a 300-character editable bio, up to
  six photos/videos with manual rearrangement, and up to five interests on
  iOS/Android (3 August 2026). Up to three editable prompts can use text or a
  paired photo (6 October 2026). [Writing your bio](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28530679977757-Writing-your-bio),
  [Uploading profile photos and videos](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28523708029341-Uploading-profile-photos-and-videos),
  [Adding your interests](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28530297182365-Adding-your-interests),
  [Adding profile prompts](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/31602664871453-Adding-profile-prompts).
- **Authentication and recovery:** The login guide documents phone verification
  codes and additional login methods (8 October 2026). The secondary-login guide
  lists Passkey, Facebook and Apple, advises adding one before changing numbers,
  and says recovery is unavailable after loss of the old number without another
  login method (3 November 2026). These sources do not establish a Bumble password
  change feature. [Signing up and logging in](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28533989291421-Signing-up-and-logging-in),
  [Secondary login methods](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28535181122461-Secondary-login-methods).
- **Pause and incognito:** Snooze offers 24 hours, 72 hours, a week or indefinite
  hiding, retains ongoing chats, and can show an away status. Unstarted chats can
  expire and subscription billing continues (4 November 2026). Incognito exposes
  the user only to people they have liked and requires Premium or Premium+
  (8 October 2026). [Taking a break](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28537106111261-Taking-a-break-from-online-dating),
  [Using incognito mode](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/30009906379165-Using-incognito-mode).
- **Context before matching:** Notes, formerly Compliments, can reference photos,
  prompts or shared interests. They are limited to 150 characters and included
  in Premium+ or purchasable separately. A Note does not unlock conversation
  without a match (10 June 2026).
  [Sending Notes](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/32450817102749-Sending-Notes).
- **Conversation changes:** The current support guide says Opening Moves have
  been removed in the updated experience. Both people can send an opening
  message; it describes 72-hour matching/reply windows and optional suggestions
  for generic openers. Updated **10 August 2026, 14:29**. The guide does not specify
  a rollout-country or app-version matrix, so this should not be generalized to
  every installed version or substituted with older 24-hour assumptions.
  [How conversations start on Bumble](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/36679912947229-How-conversations-start-on-Bumble).
- **Safety controls:** Blocking is private and irreversible; reporting can be
  omitted when blocking. Reports can be made after unmatching through Support,
  and report progress can be checked in Help Hub. Both guides updated
  3 August 2026. [Blocking someone](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28783382700701-Blocking-someone),
  [Reporting someone](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28783343585565-Reporting-someone).
- **Notifications and recommendations:** Notification controls separate messages,
  matches, tips, events and marketing/research communications
  (29 September 2026). Discover describes recommendations based on interests,
  goals, communities, profile information and prior matches; this is a general
  explanation, not proof of an individual compatibility prediction
  (31 March 2026). [Managing notifications](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28536417447837-Managing-notifications),
  [Using Discover](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28423668110621-Using-the-Discover-tab).
- **Verification and availability limits:** Photo Verification is mandatory in
  the USA, otherwise optional unless Bumble requests it for safety; automated and
  human review compare the selfie with profile photos (8 October 2026). The Share
  Date guide explicitly says the feature is **currently unavailable** while the
  experience is improved; updated **9 September 2026, 10:25**. Bumble web is
  discontinued according to its guide updated **3 August 2026, 13:02**. These
  limitations matter when comparing a browser-based app with Bumble's mobile
  product. [Verifying your photos](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28949738572829-Verifying-your-photos),
  [Sharing your date plans](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/32668227103645-Sharing-your-date-plans),
  [An update on Bumble web](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/30996192802973-An-update-on-Bumble-web).

## Hinge evidence

- **Profile expression:** Hinge requires four to six photos depending on local
  requirements and three prompt answers. Its editor supports profile details,
  individual visibility settings and a preview (28 July 2026). Prompt answers can
  be edited and reordered; three are displayed simultaneously
  (29 September 2026). The reviewed editor describes interests through prompts
  and details; it does not establish a Tinder-style free bio or universal interest
  tag picker. [Edit my profile](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360011053094-How-do-I-edit-my-profile),
  [Edit my Prompts](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311352171539-How-do-I-edit-my-Prompts).
- **Contextual Likes:** A Like can target a particular photo or prompt and include
  a comment; accepted Likes become matches. Hinge+ and HingeX have unlimited Likes,
  while the feature itself is described for ordinary members
  (24 August 2026). This documents the interaction, not the claimed improvement in
  response rates. [Likes](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311632894995-Likes).
- **Authentication and recovery:** Hinge documents phone/email verification and
  optional Google/Apple login (8 October 2026). Its phone-change article says a
  linked number cannot be changed; Support can instead offer deletion of the old
  account and recreation (23 December 2025). Security advice directs Google/Apple
  password changes to those providers (13 January 2026). A generic password-change
  feature should not be inferred from this account model.
  [Logging in / Access](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311393564563-Logging-in-Access),
  [Change my phone number](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360034066073-How-do-I-change-my-phone-number),
  [Compromised account guidance](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360010871394-I-think-my-Hinge-account-has-been-hacked).
- **Pause:** Pausing hides the profile from new discovery while preserving chats.
  Previously sent Likes can still be accepted; subscription billing does not
  pause. Guide updated 23 December 2025. No separate Hinge incognito entitlement
  was established in the reviewed sources.
  [Pausing](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360012595653-Pausing).
- **Blocking and reporting:** Reports are confidential, permanently remove mutual
  visibility and can be submitted against matches (6 October 2026). Contact-based
  Block List also permits manual entry without contact synchronization, but only
  works if supplied identifiers match the other account and does not affect
  existing matches/messages (29 April 2026). These are different operations.
  [Report someone](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/115004671227-How-do-I-report-someone),
  [Block People I Know](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/19659017273235-How-can-I-Block-People-I-Know).
- **Notifications and matching context:** The chat guide documents app/device
  notification controls and conversation-specific notifications
  (24 August 2026). Most Compatible explains general inputs as mutual preferences,
  recent activity and patterns in Likes, with a time-limited recommendation
  (27 July 2026); this is not a detailed per-pair explanation.
  [Matching and chatting](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360011090134-How-Do-I-Match-with-Someone-and-Start-Chatting),
  [Most Compatible](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360011233073-What-is-Most-Compatible).
- **Icebreakers:** Convo Starters offers ideas drawn from public profile content
  rather than drafting/sending a message. Availability is regional, users can opt
  out, and the guide describes transfer of relevant public profile data to a
  processor (7 August 2026). This is evidence for optional conversation support,
  not a recommendation to copy its AI architecture.
  [Convo Starters](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/46735258688659-What-are-Convo-Starters).
- **Verification and date safety:** Selfie Verification compares a video selfie
  with photos; changes to photos may remove the badge. It is generally voluntary,
  with required Face Check in some regions, and is not a safety guarantee
  (14 July 2026). Safe Dating Advice includes public meeting places, sharing plans
  with trusted people and independent transport; this is guidance, not evidence
  of a universal in-app date-sharing tool. [Selfie Verification](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/10303221435539-What-is-Selfie-Verification),
  [Safe Dating Advice](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360007194774-Safe-Dating-Advice).

## OkCupid evidence

The following help pages were accessed 15 November 2026. The retrieved page
bodies did not consistently expose update dates; do not treat access date as a
publication date.

- **Profile editing:** Users can edit text/photo prompts from the app or web;
  Topics provide writing prompts based on interests and hobbies. Empty prompts
  are not shown to visitors. Photos can be uploaded, reordered and removed with
  app/web-specific controls. A thumbnail crop does not remove the corresponding
  content from the full-size image. [Profile Prompts and Topics](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22770862172315-Profile-Prompts-and-Topics),
  [Upload and adjust photos](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22937864612251-How-to-upload-and-adjust-photos).
- **Introductions:** A user can Like someone and send one introduction before a
  mutual Like. Mutual interest then opens the ongoing conversation. This is a
  contextual pre-match introduction, not unrestricted cold messaging.
  [Send an introductory message](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23102978952219-How-to-send-an-introductory-message-to-someone).
- **Password and account recovery:** Forgotten-password recovery sends a login
  link to the account email; signed-in users can change their password in app or
  web settings. A changed phone number can be recovered through email/password
  login followed by a phone update. Support cannot establish ownership of a free
  account if both email access and password are lost; billing evidence may assist
  paid accounts. [Change or reset password](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22980556463643-How-to-change-or-reset-your-password),
  [SMS Verification](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23137121519771-SMS-Verification),
  [Sign-in troubleshooting](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23141441901723-Sign-in-troubleshooting).
- **Pause and incognito:** Disabling hides an account and permits reopening;
  deletion has different consequences. Incognito is a separate paid subscription
  from Basic/Premium and restricts visibility to people already liked or
  messaged. Neither is equivalent to Hinge's pause-with-existing-chat semantics.
  [Delete or disable your account](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22976833965595-How-to-delete-or-disable-your-account),
  [About Incognito](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22940218363675-About-Incognito).
- **Block/report and notifications:** Blocking and unmatching use the same
  exclusion mechanism; passing does not block. Reports can be submitted from
  profiles/messages. Notification settings provide email choices on web and
  notification toggles in the app. [Blocking and Unmatching](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23102631226395-Blocking-and-Unmatching),
  [Safety Tips](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22773332844059-Safety-Tips),
  [Editing email subscriptions/notifications](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22981193944091-Editing-email-subscriptions-notifications).
- **Explainable overlap:** Tapping the match percentage lets members compare
  questions both have answered publicly. Answers can be changed; the guide says
  they cannot be completely unanswered, though they can be made private and
  irrelevant. This supplies inspectable shared evidence rather than validating
  the score as a probability of relationship success.
  [Match Questions](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22770910347803-Match-Questions).
- **Verification and date safety:** Photo Verification uses a video selfie and
  describes deletion of facial templates within 24 hours for that process.
  A separate Face Check FAQ describes phased mandatory rollout and materially
  different retention; do not combine these into one universal policy. The age
  guide names UK Face Check and photo-based checks in Australia, Brazil and EEA.
  Safety advice includes public meetings and telling trusted people about plans;
  no universal date-sharing feature was established. [Photo Verification](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22781075719195-How-does-Photo-Verification-work),
  [Face Check FAQ](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/51602258614171-Face-Check-FAQ),
  [Age Checks](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/38538764519963-Age-Checks-at-OkCupid),
  [Safety Tips](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22773332844059-Safety-Tips).

## Practical ideas for a small privacy-conscious app

These are product judgments inferred from the patterns above, not claims that
competitor presence proves demand or that Bondi lacks the capability. Confirm
the current implementation and user needs before adding work.

1. **Finish the existing core first.** Review reliable profile saves/photo order,
   recoverable errors, accessible controls, private location presentation and
   trustworthy notification behavior. The competitor editors show that basic
   expression and explicit save/preview controls deserve attention before feature
   count. [Tinder profile editor](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003339043-Editing-your-profile),
   [Hinge profile preview](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360011053094-How-do-I-edit-my-profile).
2. **Then complete account recovery and password management.** For an
   email/password app, include forgotten-password delivery, signed-in password
   change, verified email ownership and clear invalid/expired-link feedback.
   OkCupid documents distinct recovery and signed-in change paths; the other
   credential models demonstrate why copying their UI blindly would be wrong.
   The user currently has no email service. A verified owned sending domain and
   configured mail delivery are prerequisites to real recovery; Supabase Auth
   with Resend SMTP is the selected direction, not a completed service or a finding
   of this competitor research. [OkCupid password flows](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22980556463643-How-to-change-or-reset-your-password).
3. **Choose one modest expression improvement next.** Optional short profile
   prompts or photo captions give users something specific to respond to. A
   contextual Like/comment is another candidate, but adds a new moderation and
   pre-match communication surface. Preserve mutual consent for ongoing chat and
   define how edits/deletions affect referenced content.
   [Bumble prompts](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/31602664871453-Adding-profile-prompts),
   [Hinge contextual Likes](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311632894995-Likes).
4. **Make pause and safety actions understandable.** A reversible control to hide
   a profile from new discovery can be small if its effects on prior Likes and existing
   matches are stated explicitly. Keep block, unmatch and misconduct report
   semantics distinct, including a reporting route after a conversation ends.
   The latter requires actual review ownership, not only a report button.
   [Hinge Pause](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360012595653-Pausing),
   [Bumble post-unmatch reporting](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28783343585565-Reporting-someone).
5. **Offer truthful overlap and optional starters.** Show shared public interests
   or preferences as the reason for a suggestion, using only information both
   people chose to expose. A small curated question list can support conversation
   without analyzing private messages or inferring sensitive traits. Explain
   missing data and avoid presenting a score as a success probability.
   [OkCupid public-answer comparison](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22770910347803-Match-Questions),
   [Hinge optional conversation ideas](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/46735258688659-What-are-Convo-Starters).
6. **Prefer lightweight date-safety support initially.** Provide public-meeting,
   trusted-person and independent-transport guidance at a useful moment. A later
   user-initiated plan-sharing feature needs an explicit recipient preview,
   minimal disclosed data, expiry/revocation and no automatic location tracking.
   Photo verification would require a separate privacy, retention and operations
   decision; a badge must describe its limited scope and never promise safety.
   [Hinge date guidance](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360007194774-Safe-Dating-Advice),
   [Tinder verification limitation](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/4422771431309-How-Does-Photo-Verification-Work).

## Uncertainties and verification limits

- No accounts were created, subscriptions purchased, live profiles inspected or
  app flows exercised. Device/region/subscription availability is only established
  where the cited publisher states it. In particular, Philippine availability
  remains unqualified.
- Bumble's unusually recent conversation changes, web discontinuation and
  temporarily unavailable Share Date are recorded with exact URLs and displayed
  dates above. They are the current support descriptions, not direct evidence of
  every user's installed app behavior. Recheck before relying on them in a
  release decision.
- OkCupid maintains conflicting reachable search documentation: one page says
  Match Search is redundant and describes replacement behavior; another still
  explains legacy search and A-list options. Therefore this report does not rank
  search/filter entitlements or repeat either as universally current.
  [Replacement explanation](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23110176786587-Finding-searching-and-adjusting-who-you-see),
  [Legacy Match Search guide](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23110060958491-How-to-Use-Match-Search).
- A feature not established by this bounded source review is **unverified**, not
  proven absent. This applies to Tinder prompt/comment entitlements, separate
  Hinge incognito and universal date-sharing on Hinge/OkCupid.
- Marketing claims about increased Likes, replies, dates or trust are not treated
  as independent evidence of effectiveness. No monetization, broad AI, biometric
